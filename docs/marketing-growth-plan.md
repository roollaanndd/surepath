## Executive Summary

SurePath operates in a high-potential market: Indonesia's insurance penetration rate sits at ~3% GDP, well below the ASEAN average of 5%. The digital insurance segment is growing 25%+ YoY. Winning requires owning organic search, converting WhatsApp intent, and building an agent distribution network that multiplies reach without proportional CAC.

**North Star Metrics (90-day)**
- Nasabah leads: 5,000/month by Day 90
- Agent signups: 500 new registered agents
- Quote-to-purchase CVR: lift from ~3% to 6%
- Monthly premium volume: 2× current baseline

### 1.4 WhatsApp Business Funnel

WhatsApp is the #1 communication channel in Indonesia. Build a multi-touch funnel:

```
Ad / SEO → Landing Page → "Dapatkan Penawaran via WhatsApp" CTA
                                    ↓
                         WA Business API automated welcome
                                    ↓
                    Qualification bot (jenis kendaraan, tahun, plat)
                                    ↓
                         Quote delivered in WA chat (<2 min)
                                    ↓
                    Follow-up D+1: "Ada pertanyaan tentang penawaran?"
                                    ↓
                    Follow-up D+3: Urgency message + testimonial
                                    ↓
                    Close / Handoff to agen if needed
```

### 6.1 Insurance Company Partnerships

**Partnership terms to negotiate:** Real-time API for quote + issuance, dedicated claims SLA (24-jam), co-marketing budget split 50/50, commission 10–15% of premi (industry standard digital 8–15%).

### 6.2 OJK-Compliant Affiliate Channel

**OJK SEOJK 05/2019:** Affiliates must be registered "Tenaga Pemasar" or operate under licensed intermediary. Cannot use "agen" for unlicensed affiliates — use "mitra pemasaran". All promo material pre-approved by insurer.

**Compliant affiliate structure:**
1. Register SurePath as PPAI (Perusahaan Pialang Asuransi) or partner under existing PPAI license
2. Mitra pemasaran earn: Rp 50,000–200,000 per completed application (not per polis — avoids SEOJK issue)
3. Payout only after policy issuance + OJK cooling-off period
4. All mitra register via SurePath portal with KTP verification

**Affiliate channel targets:** 20 personal finance creators (>50K followers), 15 otomotif bloggers/YouTubers, 10 travel influencers.

### 6.3 Bancassurance-Lite Concept

**Model: Embedded insurance at point of loan/card issuance**

Target banks/fintechs without full bancassurance agreements:
- **Neo-banks**: Jenius (BTPN), Jago, Blu (BCA Digital)
- **P2P Lending**: Kredivo, Akulaku — embed PA/jiwa at loan checkout
- **Auto dealer financing**: Partner with Toyota Financial, Adira Finance — embed mobil insurance at BPKB signing

**Implementation:** API-based quote at checkout; 40/60 revenue share (partner/SurePath); SurePath as licensed digital marketing agent, insurer as product issuer. Average premi Rp 2–5 juta = Rp 800K–2 juta revenue per policy.
